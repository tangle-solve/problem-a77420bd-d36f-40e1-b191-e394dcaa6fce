### CHEM-000403: Does oxygen-bound seaborgium hexaisocarbonyl exist as a metastable molecule?
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Establish whether Sg(OC)₆ is a genuine metastable minimum after correlation, spin–orbit, and vibrational effects are included. Determine its isomerization lifetime and a distinguishing experimental signature if it is viable.
- **Status:** A 2023 Dirac–Fock/Breit–Gaunt study predicted the oxygen-bound isomer while finding Sg(CO)₆ more stable. An optimized octahedral structure and favorable atomization energy leave kinetic survival unresolved.
